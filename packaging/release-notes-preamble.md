**What is new in 1.5.0.** Dictum 1.5.0 adds Meetings. It is off until you turn it on in Settings > Meetings and confirm the setup notice. Before each recording, Dictum shows what to tell everyone and asks you to confirm that each person agreed, spoken or in writing. If someone joins, recording pauses until you confirm they agreed too, and it stops at once if anyone says no. Dictum records your microphone, and your computer's sound when Windows allows it, shows a live transcript, and saves the audio in encrypted pieces as it goes. Before you start, Dictum names every service your audio or transcript will go to; summaries are written on this computer unless you choose Ollama or a cloud service with your own key. You can also import a recording once you turn imports on. Copy and Export show a sharing reminder first. Saved recordings stay until you delete them, or you can have the audio deleted after 30 or 90 days. The privacy policy on the website now has a Meetings section.

**What is new in 1.4.0.** Settings > Clean-up and Settings > Models now have Add a key, a key list and Check this key. Keys you add in Dictum are kept in Windows Credential Manager. You can keep several keys for a service and choose which one to use. Check this key sends the key to that service only when you press it, after consent; it sends nothing you dictated or wrote. Create the key file now includes the full template, and the Help page on the website has guides for getting and adding keys. Settings > Privacy can remove all keys you added in Dictum. Calibration now confirms when Use this takes effect and marks the row In use; with cloud speech on, it says the chosen model is the fallback. The voice activity gate model file is downloaded only with Dictum.exe --download-model silero-v6.2.0.

**What is new in 1.3.1.** Dictum now shows its Terms of Use (https://dictum.kynni.au/terms) the first time it starts, on every copy including one upgraded from an earlier version: the Welcome window's first step has a Continue button that accepts them, and closing the window or pressing Esc on that step quits Dictum. The installer shows a licence page, and Settings > About has Terms of use and Third-party notices buttons. The command-line modes `--transcribe` and `--bench-cleanup` need the Terms accepted once first (start Dictum and press Continue). The Privacy Policy on the website is rewritten and the Terms of Use page is new. Settings > Storage names its folders more precisely; Calibration says Downloading only once you have agreed to the download; the Offline only description names Check for updates and cloud speech; and the two cloud consent dialogs now say what hands-free mode sends with each part (for clean-up, up to ten words of what you said just before; for speech, the closing words). Qwen2.5-3B is no longer on Dictum's list of clean-up models, because its licence allows non-commercial use only. If you chose it, Dictum now uses the recommended clean-up model instead (the rule-based tidy until that is downloaded). Its 2.1 GB file stays in your models folder and Settings > Storage no longer lists it: delete `qwen2.5-3b-instruct-q4_k_m.gguf` yourself to free the space. The Gemma clean-up models now carry a note that the Gemma Terms of Use apply.

**Before you install the direct download.** It needs the Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64), version 14.51 or later. On a PC without it, speech recognition and AI clean-up cannot load and the tray shows "model error". `Dictum-Setup-<version>.exe` checks before it installs anything and offers Microsoft's download, https://aka.ms/vc14/vc_redist.x64.exe (installing it needs administrator rights). The portable zip has no installer: install the runtime first. The test MSIX, for testers (docs/TESTING-MSIX.md), needs Microsoft's Visual C++ framework package, Microsoft.VCLibs.140.00.UWPDesktop 14.0.33728.0 or later, installed first.

**The base.en speech model is included.** The installer, the zip and the test MSIX each carry `models\ggml-base.en.bin` beside `Dictum.exe` (the Whisper base.en model, MIT licence). Its notice, and those of every other third-party component, are in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`. Larger models are optional downloads from Settings > Models.
