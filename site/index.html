Dictum at a glance

Why people switch to Dictum

Most dictation tools send your voice to someone else’s servers and charge you monthly for the privilege. Dictum does the work on your own computer by default, for free.

  • Works where you work

    Email, documents, chat, browser forms, your CRM. Anywhere a cursor blinks, Dictum types there. No special editor, no copy and paste.

  • Your voice stays on your PC by default

    Speech recognition and tidy-up both run on your computer. No account, no telemetry. Cloud clean-up and cloud speech only if you turn them on. Once your models are downloaded it works with the internet off.

  • Free to keep on your PC

    No subscription, no word limits. Dictum is open source under the MIT licence, made by Kynni, a small Australian business that uses it every day.

  • Sounds like you

    The ums go, punctuation goes in, and your clients’ names are spelled your way. Calibration finds the model that hears your accent best.

The name: a dictum is a saying, something stated. From the Latin dictum, “something said”.

How it works

  1. Hold Right Ctrl

    Click where you want the words, in any app, then hold the key. A small pill at the bottom of the screen shows Dictum is listening.

  2. Say it

    Talk the way you normally would. Say “new paragraph” to start one. Talking for longer? Double-tap the key for hands-free, and press it again to stop.

  3. Let go

    Your words are typed at the cursor a moment later, tidied up. Changed your mind? Esc cancels and nothing is pasted.

Right Ctrl is the default. Pick any key you like, such as Caps Lock or an F-key, in Settings.

Dictum for Mac, in beta

The same Dictum, built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) on macOS 15 or later. It lives in your menu bar: hold the right Option key, talk, let go, and your words are typed into the app you are in. The speech model comes inside the app, so nothing is downloaded to get started, and your voice stays on your Mac unless you turn on cloud speech.

It is a beta: signed and notarised with our Apple Developer ID, so it opens like any other Mac app, but it has had fewer hours on fewer Macs than the Windows version. Unzip it, move Dictum to Applications and open it. macOS asks for Microphone access and for Accessibility access (to see your hotkey and to paste into other apps). Intel Macs are not supported. Found something? Tell us from Settings > Help.

Nothing leaves your PC, by default

Speech recognition and tidy-up run on your computer. There’s no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. Dictum goes online only when you ask it to:

  • Model downloads

    When you choose a model, Dictum fetches that one file from the model makers’ official download page and sends nothing about you. Every file Dictum downloads is checked against a fingerprint built into Dictum before it is used.

  • api.anthropic.com, api.openai.com, generativelanguage.googleapis.com Off by default

    Only if you switch on cloud tidy-up, which can use Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible server with your own key, behind a consent step. The text of each dictation is then sent there to be tidied. A server on your own PC, such as Ollama, keeps your text on it unless that server forwards to a cloud service of its own.

  • api.openai.com, {resource}.openai.azure.com, api.groq.com Off by default

    Cloud speech (off by default): the recording of each dictation goes to the service you chose, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Groq or a server you name, with your own key where the service needs one, behind its own consent step. Your dictionary words go with it as a hint. Dictum does not validate or certify how that service handles your voice.

  • dictum.kynni.au

    Only when you open the privacy policy, Dictum in plain words or the full guide from Settings, or click Check for updates in the direct-download version, which reads one small file and sends nothing about you; the Store build is updated by the Store.

  • A server you name Off by default

    Only if you point clean-up at a server of your own, such as Ollama. If the server runs on your PC, for example Ollama at localhost, nothing leaves your PC unless that server itself forwards to a cloud service, which Dictum cannot see: Ollama can serve its cloud models once you sign in to it.

Read the privacy policy. It’s short, and it names every address.

For teams

Dictum installs per user with no admin rights. It runs on laptops without a graphics card and on Windows virtual machines used over Remote Desktop, with the session’s microphone. Connecting from a Mac? Use the right Option key as your hotkey.

On a locked-down machine, switch on offline-only and copy the model files in. Nothing is sent anywhere, and your IT team can read the privacy policy and the source code.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, or an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later (beta).
  • The Microsoft Store on Windows; the Store installs everything else Dictum needs. On a Mac, the download from GitHub.
  • A microphone. Any one your computer can use.
  • 4 GB of RAM for the small models. A graphics card is optional and makes the bigger, more accurate models fast.
  • Some disk space for the models you pick: from 78 MB, up to 1.6 GB for the most accurate speech model, plus an optional 1.1 GB tidy-up model.
  • Dictates in 100 languages, English included; choose yours under Settings > Models > Spoken language. Languages other than English need a one-time model download.

Questions

Does it learn my voice?

No. Nothing is trained, and your voice stays on your PC unless you turn on cloud speech. More in the help guide.

Is the model safe?

Model files are data, not programs: they hold the numbers that whisper.cpp and llama.cpp read, and no code that runs. Dictum downloads speech models only from the whisper.cpp project’s official download page and tidy-up models only from their makers’ official pages, and checks each file against a SHA-256 fingerprint built into Dictum before using it. A download that doesn’t match is deleted, and a copied-in file that doesn’t match is refused.

The remaining risk is a bug in the model loader that a deliberately crafted file could exploit, which is what that check guards against. Two optional Gemma models need a login with the model host and can only be size-checked. If you load a model file of your own, use one you trust. The tidy-up model only ever sees the words, never your audio.

How do I remove everything?

In Dictum, open Settings, then Storage, then Remove Dictum completely. Step by step, and what Windows’ own uninstall leaves behind.
