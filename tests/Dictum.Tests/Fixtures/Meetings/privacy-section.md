## Meetings

Meetings is off until you turn it on. It saves meeting content as described below.

What is stored. Dictum saves meeting transcripts, summaries, markers and notes on your computer. It also saves audio when audio saving is on. For imports, it saves a copy of the recording; the original is not changed.

Dictum saves your agreement confirmation, its time and how you said everyone agreed. This is your note. It does not prove that anyone agreed.

Where and protection. Dictum saves meetings in the Meetings folder you choose. Dictum encrypts saved meeting content. On Windows, it stores the key in Windows Credential Manager for your Windows account.

People or harmful software using your signed-in account may be able to access meetings. Meeting encryption does not encrypt your whole drive. A folder you choose may also be copied by backup or sync software.

What leaves your computer. This depends on your settings and actions.

Cloud speech sends recorded sound to your chosen speech service.

Cloud summaries send transcript text to your chosen summary service. This includes Ollama when you choose a cloud model.

Local speech and summary processing do not send meeting sound or text to those cloud services.

Copying, exporting and team sharing put meeting content outside its original Dictum storage. Backup or sync software may also copy your Meetings folder.

Cloud services may process or store data overseas. Staff who can access shared meetings may be overseas. Provider terms and your account settings affect access, use and retention. Dictum cannot promise how other services or people protect or delete copies.

How long. There is no automatic deletion by default. You can set Dictum to delete saved audio after 30 or 90 days. Transcripts and summaries stay until you delete them. These settings do not delete original imported files or copies held by other people, cloud services, backups or sync services.

Deleting. Delete removes the meeting’s saved files from Dictum’s storage. Dictum has no undo for this action. Deletion does not guarantee that the data cannot be recovered.

It does not remove the original imported file or copies in exports, shared folders, cloud services, backups or sync services.

Your responsibilities. Rules for recording, importing and sharing can differ between places. Check the rules that apply to you and the other people involved.

Dictum requires everyone’s clear agreement. Its reminders and your confirmation do not guarantee that a recording or its use is lawful.

Dictum records what you say about agreement. Your click does not prove that anyone else agreed.

Privacy law. Dictum does not claim an exemption from the Australian Privacy Act 1988 or other privacy laws. Check the privacy duties that apply when you collect, use, store or share other people’s information. Using Dictum does not by itself meet those duties.
