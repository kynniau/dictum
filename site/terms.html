4. What Dictum does, and what we do not promise

Dictum turns your speech into text and types it where your cursor is. It can also tidy the text, with fixed rules or with optional AI clean-up. Unless you turn on a cloud option (section 6), all of this happens on your computer.

Dictum has limits:

  • Dictation is never perfect. Speech recognition mishears, and clean-up can change wording. Read what Dictum types before you rely on it, especially names, numbers and anything that matters.
  • Clean-up does not know your meaning. It tidies words; it does not understand them. Dictum checks the AI clean-up’s answer against the words it heard. If the check fails, Dictum uses the simpler rule-based tidy instead. No check catches everything.
  • It can stop working. An operating-system update, a new graphics driver, another program or a change by Microsoft or Apple can break Dictum on your computer. It stays broken until we release a fix, and we cannot promise when we will, or whether we will.
  • There is no support promise. We answer email when we can, and we fix what we can, but we do not promise a reply, a fix or a timeframe. Requests about your personal information are different: we answer those as our Privacy Policy says.
  • The website can be down. We do not promise it will always be available.
  • The Mac beta is a beta. It has had fewer hours on fewer Macs than the Windows version. Expect more problems than in the Windows version.

6. Cloud services you can choose

Dictum works without any cloud service. Two optional, advanced features send data to a service you choose. Each is off until you switch it on in Settings. Before anything leaves your computer, Dictum asks you to agree in its own consent step:

  • Cloud clean-up sends the text of each dictation to the service you chose. With it go Dictum’s clean-up instructions (including any instructions of your own), the words in your personal dictionary and, in hands-free mode, up to ten words of what you said just before.
  • Cloud speech sends the recording of each dictation, and of each recording you make in See how it works, your voice, to the service you chose. With it go your dictionary words, as a hint, and, in hands-free mode, the closing words of what you said just before.

When you turn one on:

  • Your agreement is with that service, not with us. Its terms, prices and privacy policy apply to what it receives. The service does not act for us.
  • We cannot see or control what the service does with your text or your voice. Dictum encrypts the connection to any service that is not on your computer, but cannot see, check or certify what that service keeps or does with your words. Our Privacy Policy records, with the date we checked, what each service’s own pages said about using your data to train its AI and how long it keeps it. Those pages can change; check them yourself.
  • You choose the service and what you dictate while it is on. Choose one that suits the information you dictate. Do not dictate confidential, personal or client information with a cloud option on unless you have checked the service’s terms yourself.
  • A server on your own computer (for example Ollama) is also your choice. Dictum cannot see whether that server forwards your text elsewhere.

7. Models you download

Speech and clean-up models are files of numbers, not programs. Dictum downloads one only when you choose it, from huggingface.co: speech models from the whisper.cpp project’s page, clean-up models from their makers’ pages. It checks each file it downloads against a fingerprint built into Dictum before using it.

Each model has its own licence from its maker, which applies when you use it. The speech models are OpenAI’s Whisper models as published by the whisper.cpp project, under the MIT licence; the model that ships inside Dictum (base.en) is included under that licence. The Qwen and SmolLM2 clean-up models are under the Apache-2.0 licence. The two Gemma clean-up models from Google are under Google’s Gemma Terms of Use (ai.google.dev/gemma/terms): Dictum does not download them; you download them yourself on Hugging Face after accepting Google’s terms there, and Dictum checks only their size. A model file you choose yourself by its location is checked only if it has the same file name as a model on Dictum’s list.
