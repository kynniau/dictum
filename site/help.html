Help guide

Step by step, for Dictum 1.2 on Windows. Last updated 21/10/2026.

Dictum types what you say. You hold down a key, speak, and let go. Your words appear wherever you were typing. This guide goes one step at a time. You do not need to know anything about computers to follow it.

Two things to know first

The Dictum menu. Click the Dictum icon near the clock, usually at the bottom right of the screen. If you cannot see the icon, click the small up arrow beside the clock first. The menu has Settings, Help and more.

“Settings > Models” means: open Settings from the Dictum menu, then choose Models on the left. This guide uses that shorthand for every page of Settings.

1. Get Dictum

Dictum works on Windows 10 and Windows 11 PCs. There are two ways to get it. Choose one.

The Microsoft Store (easiest)

  1. Click Get Dictum from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Follow the Store’s steps to install it.

The Store keeps Dictum up to date for you.

The direct download

  1. Click Download for Windows. The Dictum download page opens.
  2. Click the file whose name starts with Dictum-Setup. It downloads to your PC.
  3. Open your Downloads folder. Double-click Dictum-Setup.
  4. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, see the box below.
  5. Setup may say your PC needs the Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable. This is a free add-on from Microsoft that Dictum needs. Click Yes. Microsoft’s download opens. Install it too. Installing it needs an administrator account on this PC.
  6. Setup offers a desktop shortcut, and starting Dictum when Windows starts. Both are off unless you tick them. Click Next.
  7. Click Install.
  8. When it has finished, leave Launch Dictum ticked.
  9. Click Finish. Dictum starts.

“Windows protected your PC”: is this safe?

For the direct download, this message is expected. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen does not recognise it. The Microsoft Store version does not show this message, because Microsoft signs Store apps.

Only carry on if you downloaded the file from github.com/kynniau/dictum.

  1. Click More info.
  2. Click Run anyway.

If your web browser held the download back, or there is no Run anyway button, see what to click on the front page. On a work PC, your IT team may have turned these options off. Ask them, or use the Microsoft Store version instead.

2. The first time you start Dictum

The first time Dictum starts, a window called Welcome to Dictum opens. It takes you through six short steps. The top of the window says which step you are on, for example “Step 2 of 6: Model files”.

Click Next to go on. Click Back to go back.

  1. Welcome

    The window explains how Dictum works. Click Next.

  2. Model files

    A model is the file that turns speech into text. Dictum keeps its models on this PC. The one that comes with Dictum is about 150 MB (megabytes). Adding more models can take several gigabytes more.

    Dictum suggests a folder and shows how much free space that drive has.

    • To keep the suggested folder, click Next.
    • To use another drive, click Choose another drive or folder…. Pick a folder. Then click Next.

    Changed your mind? Use the suggested folder puts it back. If Dictum already knows where to keep its model files, it skips this step.

  3. Speech model

    Usually this step says “The Standard model is included and ready.” Click Next.

    If it shows choices instead, pick one. Bigger models are more accurate but slower. A model that is not on this PC yet is downloaded when you reach the end of these steps. You can change this later in Settings > Models.

  4. Your hotkey

    The hotkey is the key you hold down while you speak. Unless you change it, it is the Ctrl key on the right-hand side of the keyboard (Right Ctrl).

    1. Press the key now. The box shows its name, and a light comes on while you hold it.
    2. To choose a different key, press that key instead. A mouse button other than the left one works too.
    3. Click Use this hotkey.

  5. Microphone

    1. Choose your microphone from the list. System default suits most people.
    2. Say a few words.
    3. If it says “Heard you.”, your microphone works. Click Next.

    If it says “Nothing heard.”, check that the microphone is plugged in and not muted. Then click Test again. You can also click Skip. If Dictum hears nothing when you dictate, it will tell you.

  6. Try it

    1. Click in the practice box.
    2. Hold your hotkey.
    3. Say a sentence.
    4. Let go. Your words appear in the box.

    Dictum then explains that everyone’s accent, voice and microphone are different, and what to do if words come out wrong (see section 4). Click Finish.

If you close the window early, Dictum uses its usual settings for the steps you missed. You can change every one of them later in Settings.

3. Dictate: hold, speak, let go

  1. Click where you want the words to go, for example in an email.
  2. Hold down your hotkey. Unless you changed it, this is the Ctrl key on the right-hand side of the keyboard.
  3. Speak in your normal voice. A small bar above the taskbar shows that Dictum is listening.
  4. Let go of the key. A moment later, your words appear where you clicked.

Unless you turned clean-up off, Dictum puts in punctuation and capital letters, and takes out ums and ahs. Say “new paragraph” to start a new paragraph.

Talking for a while? Use hands-free

  1. Tap the hotkey twice, quickly. Dictum keeps listening without you holding the key.
  2. Speak.
  3. Tap the hotkey once more to stop.

Changed your mind? Press Esc

Press Esc before your words appear, while you are holding the key or just after you let go. Nothing is typed. In hands-free, Esc stops listening, and what is already typed stays.

Turn Dictum off for a while

Open the Dictum menu and use the Dictation on switch.

4. If Dictum gets your words wrong

Everyone’s voice, accent and microphone are different, so a speech model hears some people better than others. The speech model that comes with Dictum is a good place to start. If words often come out wrong, try these, in order.

Check your microphone

  1. Keep the microphone close to your mouth. Speak at your normal speed.
  2. Open Settings > Microphone.
  3. Choose your microphone from the list.
  4. Click Test microphone and speak. The bars move when Dictum hears you.

Find the model that hears you best

  1. Open the Dictum menu.
  2. Click Calibrate my voice.
  3. Dictum shows a short sentence. Click Record, then read it aloud in your normal voice.
  4. Do the same for each sentence. There are ten.
  5. Dictum tells you which of the speech models on this PC hears you best.

Calibrate my voice measures the models. It does not change them. The recordings it makes stay on this PC until you delete them.

Choose a more accurate model

  1. Open Settings > Models.
  2. Find More accurate.
  3. Click Download… next to it. If it is already on this PC, the button says Use.
  4. When it has finished, it says In use.

Bigger models are more accurate, but slower, and each one is a download you choose. Most accurate needs a strong PC, or patience. On a PC without a graphics card (the part of a computer that draws the screen, also called a GPU), it can take longer than you spoke.

Teach Dictum a word

If a name or word keeps coming out wrong, see how to add words and spellings.

5. Simple and Advanced

At the top of Settings > Models there is a switch with two sides: Simple and Advanced. Dictum starts on Simple.

  • Simple shows what each choice means.
  • Advanced shows every model and its details.

What Simple shows

For the speech model, up to three choices:

  • Standard. Included with Dictum. Works well for most people.
  • More accurate. A bigger download, and a bit slower.
  • Most accurate. Best results. Needs a strong PC, or patience.

What each line says depends on your PC. On a PC without a graphics card, Simple says so: the bigger choices take longer there.

For clean-up (the step that tidies your words after they are heard), two choices:

  • Tidy my words (recommended). Fixes punctuation and removes ums and ahs. Runs on this PC.
  • Off. Pastes exactly what was heard.

Each choice has a button on the right. Use switches to it. Download… fetches it first. In use means it is the one Dictum is using now.

What Advanced adds

Advanced lists every speech model and clean-up model, with its details. It also lets you choose a model file of your own. Most people never need it.

6. Where the model files live

Settings > Models shows the Model folder. This is where Dictum keeps the models you download. The speech model that comes with Dictum is about 150 MB and is kept with the program itself.

Bigger speech models and AI clean-up are downloads you choose. If you add the largest, most accurate ones, they can take up several GB (gigabytes). Settings > Storage lists every model with its size and its folder.

Keep the models on another drive

  1. Open Settings > Models.
  2. Scroll down to Model folder.
  3. Click Browse….
  4. Choose a folder on the other drive, for example D:\Dictum models.
  5. Click Select Folder.

New downloads now go to that folder. Changing the folder does not move any files. Models you already downloaded stay where they are, and Dictum still finds them there.

To free up space on the first drive, copy the models across yourself, or download them again. Then delete the old copies in Settings > Storage, with Delete… next to each one.

A folder on a network drive works, but models load slowly from it. A folder on this PC is faster. To go back to the usual folder, click Use default.

7. Your privacy

Dictum turns your voice into text on this PC, then throws the recording away. Nothing you say is sent anywhere unless you turn on cloud clean-up, and it is off unless you turn it on. In the words of our privacy policy:

We collect nothing. Dictum has no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. We never receive your audio or your text.

Read the privacy policy. It is short, and it names every address.

8. Free

Free: no subscription, no account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry.

The base product stays free to keep on your PC; optional paid extras may come later.

Dictum is open source, which means anyone can read the program’s code. It is made by Kynni Pty Ltd and shared under the MIT licence.

9. Send feedback or report a problem

Dictum sends nothing by itself. Instead, it opens a new email in your own email program. You can read and change it before you send it.

Send feedback or suggest a feature

  1. Open the Dictum menu.
  2. Click Help.
  3. Click Send feedback or suggest a feature. A new email opens, with the version of Dictum, your Windows build and the models in use filled in.
  4. Write what you would like to say.
  5. Send it.

Report a problem

  1. Open Settings > About.
  2. Click Report a problem. A new email opens, with the version and models filled in. Nothing you dictated is attached.
  3. Say what happened, and what you expected.
  4. Send it.

Email support on the Help page opens the same email.

No email program?

Write to hello@kynni.com.au from any email account. Tell us which version of Dictum you have. It is shown in Settings > About.

10. Questions and answers

Click a question to see its answer.

Where does my voice go?

Nowhere. Dictum turns your voice into text on this PC, then throws the recording away. It never saves your voice to a file and never sends it anywhere.

The only recordings Dictum keeps are the ones you make on purpose in Calibrate my voice. They stay on this PC until you delete them.

If you turn on cloud clean-up (it is off unless you turn it on), the text of each dictation is sent to the service you chose. Your voice still stays here.

Why did it type the wrong words?

Dictum can mishear words, just as people can. These things help:

  • Keep the microphone close to your mouth, and speak at your normal speed.
  • If a name or word keeps coming out wrong, add it to the dictionary.
  • To teach Dictum a fix, use Fix last dictation in the Dictum menu.
  • For better accuracy overall, see If Dictum gets your words wrong.
It says “Mic error” or “No sound from mic”, or nothing happens when I talk. What do I check?

If Dictum shows “No sound from mic”, it says: Dictum is not hearing anything. Check that the microphone is plugged in and not muted. On a remote or virtual desktop, microphone redirection must be on.

If Dictum cannot find a microphone at all, it says: No microphone found. Plug one in, or on a remote or virtual desktop turn on microphone redirection, then try again.

  • Check the microphone is plugged in, switched on and not muted. Many headsets have a mute button.
  • Open Settings > Microphone. Choose your microphone from the list, then choose Test microphone and speak. The bars move when Dictum hears you.
  • In Windows Settings, go to Privacy & security > Microphone. “Microphone access” and “Let desktop apps access your microphone” must both be on.
  • On a remote or virtual desktop (a computer you use over the internet or your office network), your own microphone has to be passed through to it. This is called microphone redirection, and it must be turned on. If you are not sure how, ask whoever looks after your computers.
Nothing happens when I press the key. What do I check?
  • Open the Dictum menu. Check that the Dictation on switch is on.
  • Check which key Dictum is listening for. Open Settings > General. The Press your hotkey box shows exactly what Windows receives when you press a key.
  • Windows does not let Dictum type into a program running as administrator (with extra permissions). Try another program, such as Notepad.
  • Open Settings > About and click Reload settings. This also sets up the hotkey again if Windows removed it.
My keyboard has no Ctrl key on the right. What do I do?

Choose another key. See How do I change the key I hold?

On a Mac over Remote Desktop use the right Option key (RightAlt) or right Command (RightWin).

How do I change the key I hold?
  1. Open Settings > General.
  2. Under Press your hotkey (the hotkey is the key you hold), choose Change….
  3. Press the key you want.
  4. When Dictum shows its name, choose Use this hotkey.

Most keys work, and so do the side buttons on a mouse. The Fn key, Esc and Tab cannot be used, and neither can Enter or Space on their own.

How do I stop it listening?

Let go of the key. Dictum stops listening and types what you said.

To cancel instead, press Esc before your words appear, while you are holding the key or just after you let go. Nothing is typed. The program you are typing in also receives the Esc.

If you started hands-free (by tapping the key twice quickly), tap the key once more to stop. Esc also stops hands-free, and what is already typed stays.

To turn Dictum off for a while, use the Dictation on switch in the Dictum menu.

Can I use it in Word, Outlook, Teams or a web browser?

Yes. Dictum types into almost any program where you can type, including Word, Outlook, Teams and web browsers. Click where the words should go first.

The exception is a program running as administrator (with extra permissions): Windows does not let Dictum type into it.

Dictum closed straight away when I opened it. What do I do?

On some PCs, Dictum 1.1.1 closed as soon as it started. Newer versions have the fix. Install the latest version from the Microsoft Store, or from this website.

If the Dictum menu, or Settings > About, shows a button called Try on the CPU, choose it. Dictum then runs the speech model on the computer’s main chip (the CPU) instead of the graphics card. This can be slower.

If it still closes, email hello@kynni.com.au and tell us.

Does Dictum work on a virtual desktop, or on a PC with no graphics card?

Yes. A virtual desktop is a computer you use over the internet or your office network. The Microsoft Store listing says:

Runs on PCs without a graphics card and on Windows virtual machines over Remote Desktop with microphone redirection.

The note for Dictum 1.1.2 says:

Fixes Dictum closing straight away on PCs without a working graphics driver, such as virtual desktops.

On a virtual desktop, microphone redirection must be on. See the microphone question. Without a graphics card, the bigger models are slower. Standard is the quickest.

Which languages can I speak?

English, or any of the languages Whisper supports. Whisper is the speech technology Dictum uses.

  1. Open Settings > Models.
  2. Find Language.
  3. Open the Spoken language list. It shows every language you can choose.
  4. Choose yours.

Anything but English needs a model that understands more than one language. In the Simple view, the choices change to suit the language you picked. Translate to English, in the same place, writes English whatever language you speak.

How do I teach Dictum a name, or how to spell a word?

Add a word to the dictionary. Use this for a name, product or term that Dictum should spell your way.

  1. Open the Dictum menu.
  2. Click Dictionary. The Add a word window opens.
  3. Type the word or phrase.
  4. Click Add. It is used from the next dictation.

Teach Dictum a fix. Use this when Dictum keeps writing one word instead of another.

  1. Open the Dictum menu.
  2. Click Fix last dictation.
  3. Correct the text.
  4. Tick the changes you want kept.
  5. Click Save corrections. Dictum makes that fix from then on.
What does clean-up do? Can I turn it off?

Clean-up tidies your words after Dictum hears them. You choose it in Settings > Models, under Clean-up model.

  • Tidy my words (recommended). Fixes punctuation and removes ums and ahs. Runs on this PC.
  • Add AI clean-up. A bigger tidy-up model, about 1.1 GB to download, that also runs on this PC.
  • Off. Pastes exactly what was heard.
What is cloud clean-up? Is it safe?

Cloud clean-up is an advanced option. It is off unless you turn it on, in Settings > Clean-up, after a consent step. While it is on, the text of every dictation is sent to the service you chose. Your voice still stays on this PC.

Dictum shows this warning while it is on:

Advanced option: your text leaves this PC. Dictum does not validate or certify how that service handles it.

Most people do not need it. Tidy my words runs on this PC.

Does Dictum need the internet?

No. The Standard speech model comes with Dictum, so you can dictate with the internet off.

Dictum goes online only when you ask it to: to download a model you choose, to check for updates in the direct-download version, or to use cloud clean-up if you turn it on. Offline only, in Settings > Privacy, stops all of these.

Does Dictum learn my voice?

No. Nothing is trained, and your voice never leaves this PC. Calibrate my voice measures which model hears you best. It does not change the model.

What Dictum does keep is your dictionary and your corrections, on this PC.

Where are the model files, and how big are they?

Settings > Models shows the Model folder, where Dictum keeps the models you download. The speech model that comes with Dictum is about 150 MB (megabytes) and is kept with the program itself.

Bigger speech models and AI clean-up are downloads you choose. If you add the largest, most accurate ones, they can take up several GB (gigabytes).

To keep them on another drive, see Where the model files live. Settings > Storage lists every model with its size, and you can delete the ones you do not need there.

How do I get updates?

From the Microsoft Store: updates come from the Microsoft Store. You do not need to do anything.

From the direct download:

  1. Open Settings > About.
  2. Click Check for updates. Dictum checks only when you click it.
  3. If a newer version is out, click Open the download page.
  4. Download and run the new Setup, as in section 1. Your settings and models are kept.
How do I remove Dictum completely?
  1. Open Settings > Storage.
  2. At the bottom, choose Remove Dictum completely…
  3. Dictum lists everything it will delete, with sizes. Read it.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

It deletes its models, recordings, logs, settings, dictionary, corrections, snippets and saved keys, its Start menu shortcut and its start-with-Windows entry, then closes. If you got Dictum from the Microsoft Store, Windows Settings then opens so you can uninstall the app itself. Otherwise Dictum removes the program too, unless its first warning says it cannot.

If I uninstall Dictum the usual Windows way, what does it leave behind?

Microsoft Store version: Windows deletes Dictum’s data when you uninstall it.

Direct download: uninstalling from Windows Settings > Apps asks whether to delete your models, settings and recordings too. If you say no, they stay on this PC.

If you chose a model folder on another drive, Settings > Storage shows which models are in it. Delete them there first if you no longer want them. To delete everything in one step, see How do I remove Dictum completely?

Where is the log file?

The log is a record of what Dictum did, which helps when something goes wrong. It stays on this PC.

  1. Open Settings > About.
  2. Click Open log folder. There is one file for each day.

The log never contains what you said, unless you turn on Write dictated text to the log in Settings > Privacy. It is off unless you turn it on.

Is Dictum free?

Yes. There is no charge, no subscription and no word limit. See Free.

Dictum is open source, which means anyone can read the program’s code. It is made by Kynni Pty Ltd and shared under the MIT licence.

What does the name Dictum mean?

The name: a dictum is a saying, something stated. From the Latin dictum, “something said”.

Is there help inside Dictum itself?

Yes. Open the Dictum menu and click Help. It answers the most common questions, and it works without the internet.
