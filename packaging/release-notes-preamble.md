**What is new in 1.3.1.** Dictum now shows its Terms of Use (https://dictum.kynni.au/terms) the first time it starts, on every copy including one upgraded from an earlier version: the Welcome window's first step has a Continue button that accepts them, and closing the window or pressing Esc on that step quits Dictum. The installer shows a licence page, and Settings > About has Terms of use and Third-party notices buttons. The command-line modes `--transcribe` and `--bench-cleanup` need the Terms accepted once first (start Dictum and press Continue). The Privacy Policy on the website is rewritten and the Terms of Use page is new. Settings > Storage shows where Dictum's data folder is; Calibration says Downloading only once you have agreed to the download; the Offline only description names Check for updates and cloud speech; and the two cloud consent dialogs say that in hands-free mode the closing words of what you said just before go with the next part. Qwen2.5-3B is no longer on Dictum's list of clean-up models, because its licence allows non-commercial use only. If you chose it, Dictum now uses the recommended clean-up model instead (the rule-based tidy until that is downloaded). Its 2.1 GB file stays in your models folder and Settings > Storage no longer lists it: delete `qwen2.5-3b-instruct-q4_k_m.gguf` yourself to free the space. The Gemma clean-up models now carry a note that the Gemma Terms of Use apply.

**Before you install the direct download.** It needs the Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64), version 14.51 or later. On a PC without it, speech recognition and AI clean-up cannot load and the tray shows "model error". `Dictum-Setup-<version>.exe` checks before it installs anything and offers Microsoft's download, https://aka.ms/vc14/vc_redist.x64.exe (installing it needs administrator rights). The portable zip has no installer: install the runtime first. The test MSIX, for testers (docs/TESTING-MSIX.md), needs Microsoft's Visual C++ framework package, Microsoft.VCLibs.140.00.UWPDesktop 14.0.33728.0 or later, installed first.

**The base.en speech model is included.** The installer, the zip and the test MSIX each carry `models\ggml-base.en.bin` beside `Dictum.exe` (the Whisper base.en model, MIT licence). Its notice, and those of every other third-party component, are in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`. Larger models are optional downloads from Settings > Models.
